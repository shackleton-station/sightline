## If it's slides instead of video

1. **The problem:** your landing slipped; is the site still viable?
2. **Why it's hard:** panorama with the Sun at the horizon.
3. **The answer in seconds:** landing windows at Connecting Ridge, closed March 9 to June 21.
4. **Finding an alternative:** Peak near Shackleton opens April 1.
5. **Anywhere on the Moon:** Apollo 11 vs the pole, plus the map.
6. **Why trust it:** Horizons match, NASA map comparison, and the ridge gap stated openly.
7. **Who it's for, and what's next.**

## Recording notes

- **Show real screens.** A screen recording isn't AI-generated, but any AI-generated image, video or voice needs a
  visible "AI-generated" mark.
- **No NASA logos or insignia,** and don't imply NASA endorses the tool.
- **Caption the example limits** in the 11–23 s shots: 14-day mission, 72 h without Sun or Earth.
- **"Checked against NASA data" should stay honest.** If someone asks, the answer is `docs/VALIDATION.md`, including
  the ridge-top gap.
- **Record on desktop width,** where the panorama is largest. Then check it's readable on a phone.
