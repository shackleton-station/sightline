What the site is

The site is a demonstration. Its figures are computed from public NASA data and are checked, but they are not approved for planning a real mission. It is not affiliated with or endorsed by NASA. Data sources are listed in the project's references.

Using it

  • The site is free and needs no account.
  • Don't try to break it, overload it, or get around its request limits.
  • Don't put personal information, or anything unlawful or abusive, into plans or questions. Saved plans can be opened by anyone with the link.
  • Answers from the co-pilot are written by an AI model from the site's own figures. They can still be wrong; check the numbers on the page.
